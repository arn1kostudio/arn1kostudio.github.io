ArnEQ Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-04-09

Overview

ArnEQ is an Android app by Arn1ko Studio that lets users manage equalizer presets and sound effect settings on their device.

Data collection

ArnEQ does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

The app does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash reporting SDKs, user accounts, or cloud sync.

Data stored on the device

ArnEQ stores settings locally on the user's device using Android SharedPreferences, including the selected sound profile, equalizer band values, app theme, app language, effect toggles, and current app state.

This data stays on the device and is not sent to external servers.

Permissions used

  • android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS for applying volume and audio effect settings.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS for showing the foreground service notification when background mode is enabled.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE and android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK for keeping the sound profile active in background mode.

Third-party services

ArnEQ does not currently connect to third-party servers or APIs for user data processing.

Children's privacy

ArnEQ is a utility app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

User controls

  • Users can change or reset app settings inside the app.
  • Users can disable background mode at any time.
  • Users can uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data.
