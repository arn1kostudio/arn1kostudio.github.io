Play and privacy

Progress is saved on your device. Puzzles work offline. With a connection, you can choose rewarded ads for hints or nuts. Interstitial ads may appear between levels after every five completions. Advertising uses Google AdMob and applicable consent choices.

No player account is required. Android 7.0 or later, on supported 64-bit devices.

One gravity, three worlds

Explore the Pocket Constructor, the Alchemist's Workshop, and the Orbital Workshop. Each chapter brings its own atmosphere as you guide every free gear with a single swipe.
