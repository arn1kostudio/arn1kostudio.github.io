ArnVek Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 19, 2026.

Developer: Arn1ko Studio · arn1kostudios@gmail.com

1. Scope

This policy explains how the Android game ArnVek (package com.arnvek.game) handles information. It applies to ArnVek, rather than to every application in the studio portfolio. ArnVek does not require a player account.

2. Information saved on your device

ArnVek saves level progress, best results, stars, in-game currency, hint inventory, rewards, cosmetic choices, achievements, daily-reward dates, the counter of completed levels used for ad frequency, the current campaign attempt, and preferences on your device. These game saves are not uploaded to a server operated by Arn1ko Studio. Google Play Games cloud-save integration is not currently included.

3. Google AdMob and advertising data

ArnVek includes Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. You can choose a rewarded ad for an in-game bonus, such as hints or nuts. Interstitial ads may be displayed between levels after every five successful completions on the device. The puzzles can also be played offline; ads require a connection and availability.

The advertising SDK and its service providers may collect and process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • advertising and other device identifiers, where available and permitted;
  • device, operating-system, app, and network information;
  • ad requests, impressions, interactions, video views, and related app interactions; and
  • diagnostic and performance information, including SDK errors and crash information.

Google and its advertising partners process this information to deliver and measure ads, provide the advertising service, and detect abuse or fraud, under their applicable policies and your privacy choices. AdMob provides the developer with advertising reports. This SDK processing is separate from local game saves.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services.

4. Consent and privacy choices

ArnVek uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update consent information and, where required and available, display Google's consent form before requesting ads. When the SDK requires a privacy-options entry, it is available in the game settings. You can use it to review or change the choices Google makes available for your region. Ad requests remain disabled while the consent status is unknown or required consent has not been completed.

Android may also offer advertising-ID and ad-privacy controls in the device settings. Disabling or restricting personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads or all technical data processing by the advertising provider.

5. Permissions and purchases

The game uses network access for advertising and linked online pages, vibration for optional feedback, and advertising-related identifiers and technical network/device information where provided by Android and the SDK. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise GPS location. Real-money in-app purchases are not enabled in this release.

6. Support

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use that correspondence to answer your request and investigate the reported issue. Sending a support message is optional and is not required to play.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage in Android or uninstall the game. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete those local saves. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Data processed by Google and advertising partners is subject to their retention rules and deletion mechanisms; clearing ArnVek's local storage does not erase information already processed by those providers.

8. Children and privacy requests

The game does not ask players for a date of birth. Parents or guardians with questions about a child's information can contact us. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, or withdraw consent. Contact us for requests concerning information we hold, such as support correspondence. Requests about information held by Google may also need to be directed to Google.

9. Security, external pages, and updates

We use reasonable safeguards for information we handle. Advertising data is sent using the SDK's network mechanisms; no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose operator may process technical connection information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Other linked sites have their own policies.

We may update this policy when the game or its data handling changes. The updated policy will be published here with a revised date.
