Privacy Policy

Arn Net Scanner

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Overview

Arn Net Scanner is an Android utility for discovering devices on a local Wi-Fi or LAN network, checking common ports, and running ping tests. This policy explains what information the app processes and how it is handled.

Information processed by the app

To provide its core features, the app may process local network information such as the device's local IP address, gateway, Wi-Fi network name, discovered device IP addresses, hostnames, open ports, response times, and scan or ping results. The app sends network probes only as part of a scan or ping requested by the user.

This information is processed on the device. Arn1ko Studio does not operate a server that receives the network scan results, device addresses, hostnames, port results, or ping results from the app.

Location permission

Android may require location permission for the app to read the name of the current Wi-Fi network. Arn Net Scanner uses this permission only for network metadata and does not use GPS to determine your physical location. The app does not disclose your precise physical location to Arn1ko Studio or to other users.

Information stored locally

The app may store settings and recent scan history on the device, including language, theme, scan speed, hostname and port preferences, ping preferences, scan ranges, timestamps, and summary results. This information is not uploaded to Arn1ko Studio. It can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Notifications and background scanning

If you use background scanning, Android may show a foreground-service notification and keep the scan running while the app is not visible. Notification and battery-optimization permissions are used only to support this feature. They are not used for advertising or tracking.

External apps and local device links

When you choose an action such as opening an HTTP, HTTPS, or RTSP address, Android may open another app on your device. That external app or local device may process the address and network traffic under its own policies. Arn1ko Studio does not control third-party apps, routers, or devices on your network.

Data sharing and advertising

Arn Net Scanner does not require an account and does not sell, advertise based on, or share the app's locally processed network information with the developer or other users. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in its core workflow.

Security

The app is designed to keep scan data on the device. You are responsible for the security of your device, Wi-Fi network, router, and any local devices you scan. Do not scan networks or devices without authorization.

Children

Arn Net Scanner is a general network utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, permissions, or distribution requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
